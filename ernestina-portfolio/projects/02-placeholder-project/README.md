# Project 02: Budget vs Actual Variance Analysis

## Overview
This project analyses Budget versus Actual financial performance across key departments of a retail business in Ghana. The goal is to calculate variances, identify areas of over- and under-spending, and provide practical management recommendations.

## Business Context
Budget vs Actual analysis is a core responsibility of management accountants and finance professionals. This project demonstrates the ability to turn financial data into clear insights that support better decision-making and cost control.

## Objectives
- Calculate and interpret budget variances (Favourable and Adverse)
- Identify departments with the largest overruns and savings
- Analyse monthly variance trends
- Provide actionable recommendations for management

## Tools Used
- Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn)
- Google Colab
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
Synthetic but realistic monthly Budget vs Actual data covering 8 months and 8 departments/cost centres:
- Sales Revenue
- Cost of Goods Sold
- Staff Costs
- Rent & Utilities
- Marketing & Advertising
- Transport & Logistics
- Administrative Expenses
- Depreciation

## Key Insights
1. Depreciation recorded the largest adverse variance (most over budget).
2. Administrative Expenses showed the largest favourable variance (under budget).
3. Several cost centres experienced repeated adverse variances.
4. Monthly total variance fluctuated, showing uneven budget performance over the period.

## Recommendations
1. Investigate the significant adverse variance in Depreciation to determine whether it is due to new asset purchases or inaccurate budgeting.
2. Document and maintain the cost-control practices that led to the favourable variance in Administrative Expenses.
3. Strengthen the monthly budget review process for departments with consistent overruns.
4. Introduce early-warning variance reports so management can act before problems escalate.
5. Review the budgeting methodology for variable cost centres such as Marketing and Transport to improve accuracy.
